THE EXPLORER'S FIELD GUIDE

The grammar of tomorrow.

Both talk about the future. The difference is why you're talking about it.

01 / WILL

Decide now. Promise. Predict.

SUBJECT + WILL + BASE VERB

Spontaneous decision: “I'm thirsty. I'll get some water.”

Promise or offer: “I will help you.”

Opinion or belief: “I think we will win.”

Negative: won't · Question: Will you come?

02 / BE GOING TO

Plan ahead. See the signs.

AM / IS / ARE + GOING TO + VERB

Previous plan: “I'm going to study tonight.”

Visible evidence: “Look at the clouds! It's going to rain.”

Intention: “She's going to learn guitar.”

Negative: isn't going to · Question: Are you going to go?

✧ THE SECRET: WILL = NOW / OPINION · GOING TO = BEFORE / EVIDENCE

THE ANCIENT RULES

Survive the expedition.

01

Two explorers, one path.

Take turns. The youngest explorer begins. You can play together on the same device.

02

Roll the ancient die.

The die chooses how many spaces you can move (1–6).

03

Answer the jungle's challenge.

Choose WILL or BE GOING TO. Correct? Move forward. Wrong? Stay where you are. Every card explains why.

04

Watch out for the wilds.

Golden spaces move you forward. Red spaces pull you back. Reach space 18 in Short Quest, or 36 in Full Adventure, to win.

TIP: Press SPACE to roll, A or B to answer, ENTER to continue.
